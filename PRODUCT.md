## Platform

web

## Users

Biomedical engineers and institutional administrators in healthcare and veterinary organizations (IPS, clinics, consulting offices, laboratories, veterinary centers) who manage medical equipment and need to centralize inventory, lifecycle tracking, maintenance scheduling, and regulatory compliance.

## Product Purpose

Novamed Ingeniería provides a platform for managing biomedical technology: centralizing inventory, equipment records (hojas de vida), maintenance schedules, document management, QR-based traceability, work orders, and compliance indicators in a single place. Success means the institution has a clear, updated view of all its biomedical technology and can demonstrate compliance.

## Positioning

An integrated platform plus hands-on services model: the software connects all equipment lifecycle data while Novamed simultaneously offers the maintenance, metrology verification, and commercialization services that the platform tracks. A competitor could build software OR offer services, but not both in a connected loop.

## Operating Context

- Institutions manage fleets of biomedical equipment across multiple categories
- Regulatory compliance requires documented maintenance, calibration, and verification records
- Equipment identification via QR codes enables field access to lifecycle data
- Work orders connect maintenance activities to specific equipment and responsible parties
- Demo scheduling and WhatsApp are primary conversion channels
- Site serves Colombian market (Cali-based, Spanish language)

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Equipment inventory with connected traceability across the platform
- QR code generation and scanning for instant equipment access
- Equipment records (hojas de vida) with full documentation
- Maintenance scheduling (preventive, corrective, predictive)
- Metrology verification tracking (pressure, temperature, humidity, weight, flow, volume, angular speed, energy, length, oxygen saturation)
- Work order management and assignment
- Document management with compliance indicators
- Dashboard and KPIs
- Alert system for expirations and deadlines
- Four pricing tiers: Lite ($36,900/mo, 1 user, 20 assets), Basic ($69,900/mo, 3 users, 150 assets), Pro ($149,900/mo, 6 users, 500 assets), Enterprise ($299,900/mo, 10 users, 2,000 assets)
- Complementary services: maintenance, metrology, commercialization of biomedical equipment
